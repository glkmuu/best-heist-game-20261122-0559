THE GILDED HOUR

One thief. Three floors. Every vault in the tower — before the alarm fills.

How to play
WASD / arrows — move  ·  Shift — sneak (slow, quiet, hard to spot)
E — pick up loot  ·  Q — toss a coin to distract a guard
H — hint (exit arrow, 3 per level)  ·  R — restart level
P — pause  ·  M — mute

Guards patrol with vision cones — walls and cover block their sight. Cameras sweep. Lasers trip the alarm unless you crawl under them. Lift all the loot to unlock the exit, then slip out. Fill the alarm meter and you are caught — 3 lives.

An original game — art, levels and sounds made for this bounty.
